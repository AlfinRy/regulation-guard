# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Legal and compliance teams at mid-to-large companies in Indonesia and ASEAN: BUMN, Tbk companies, fintech, healthtech. General Counsel managing many vendor contracts simultaneously. They are time-pressed professionals who need to trust the tool immediately, not explore it.

## Product Purpose

RegulationGuard is a multi-agent compliance review system that takes a contract or policy document, runs it through 4 specialized AI agents (Policy Reader, Risk Analyzer, Legal Cross-Checker, Compliance Reporter), and produces an audit report with risk scores, regulation citations, and remediation steps. The landing page exists to communicate this capability to hackathon judges, potential users, and investors, and to drive them toward trying the product.

## Brand Personality

Precise, technical, authoritative. Three words: surgical, systematic, unambiguous. The tool feels like a well-calibrated instrument, not a friendly assistant.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages with hero-metric big numbers and gradient accents
- Warm beige/cream backgrounds
- Corporate "enterprise" feel with rounded cards and stock photos
- Marketing buzzwords and aspirational copy
- Anything that looks like a generic compliance dashboard template

## Design Principles

1. **Show the machine.** The 4-agent pipeline is the product's core differentiator. The landing page should make the process visible: input flows through agents and produces output, like watching a terminal execute commands.
2. **Terminal-native confidence.** The audience reads legal documents for a living. They respect precision and structure over decoration. Monospace type, code-block layouts, and data-dense tables signal competence, not coldness.
3. **Structured over decorated.** Borders, dividers, and grid lines create visual order. Every element has a clear boundary. No floating cards, no blurred backgrounds, no decorative gradients.
4. **Data as proof.** Risk scores, confidence percentages, regulation IDs, and agent status indicators are the content, not decoration. Present them with the density and formatting of a technical readout.
5. **One pipeline, one story.** The page tells a single narrative: upload a document, watch agents work, get a report. Every section advances that story.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast on all text against dark backgrounds
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion for scanner line and any animations
- Keyboard-navigable throughout
- Screen-reader friendly with semantic landmarks and labels
